About SAU POOL

SAU POOL is a student-focused platform designed especially for students of South Asian University. It helps students discover nearby restaurants, cafes, shopping stores, salons and other services around Chhattarpur and nearby South Delhi.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make student life easier and more affordable by connecting students with local businesses offering student-friendly discounts.

Why SAU POOL?

Students often have to search different social media pages or ask friends to find nearby offers. SAU POOL brings categories, local business information and sample offers together in one place.

How to Use the Website

Choose a category, search for a business, check its location and ask the business to confirm its current offer before visiting. Carry your SAU student ID card for in-person verification where accepted.

Our Goal

We want to make it easier for students to discover local places, save money, enjoy their university life and support nearby businesses.
